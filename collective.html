How It Works

What is it

MORE THAN JUST A
MEMBERSHIP

The Happening Collective is our loyalty programme — a way to reward the students who make THS what it is. Every time you attend a class or workshop, you earn a stamp.

Collect enough stamps and you unlock exclusive benefits — discounts, priority booking, free classes, merch, and access to events that aren't open to everyone.

This isn't a points system or a card you forget in your wallet. It's a digital pass on your phone — always with you, always up to date.

Every class.
Every workshop.
One stamp closer.

Your loyalty lives on your phone. Each time you show up, your trainer stamps your pass. Watch your benefits unlock in real time.

The Process

HOW IT
WORKS

01

Join The Collective

Enroll as a regular batch student at THS. Your digital wallet pass is created automatically and sent to you via WhatsApp or Instagram.

02

Save Your Pass

Add it to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet with one tap. It lives on your lock screen — no app needed, no login required.

03

Show Up & Get Stamped

Every class, your trainer stamps your pass. Workshop attendance counts too. Each stamp is added to your pass instantly.

04

Unlock Benefits

Hit stamp milestones and benefits unlock automatically. From discounts to free classes — the more you show up, the more you get.

Questions

FAQS

Is The Happening Collective free to join? +

Yes — completely free. If you're a regular batch student at THS, you're automatically eligible. There's no extra fee to join the programme.

Do workshop attendees get stamps too? +

Yes. Attending a THS workshop counts as a stamp. The more workshops you attend, the faster you move up tiers.

What if I take a break from classes? +

Your stamps don't expire. If you take a break and come back, your pass picks up right where you left off. We reward loyalty over time, not just recent activity.

How do I add the pass to my phone wallet? +

When your pass is created, you'll receive a link. Tap it on your iPhone to add to Apple Wallet, or on Android to add to Google Wallet. One tap, done — it lives on your lock screen.

Can I use my benefits on the same day they unlock? +

Yes. Benefits are applied to your account as soon as you hit the milestone. Discounts apply to your next booking immediately.

Do I need an app? +

No app needed. Your pass lives natively in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet — the same place your boarding passes and metro cards live. No download, no account, no login.
